My perspective

I get it

I’ve worked in scrappy startups and inside the well-resourced giants of the S&P 500. Both have the same goal: growth. And both wrestle with the same thing: constraint. The giants drown in data and insights; the scrappy startups never feel like they have enough. But in my experience, the best creative unlocks come when you embrace your constraint.

Leading teams to focus with clarity and conviction is the kind of work that makes me come alive. It’s why I’m excited by the Director of Marketing role at Holy Post Media.

Operating at the edge of growth takes someone who can dig into the nitty-gritty details and lead from the ten-thousand-foot view. I’ve led in both places, and this feels like the exact challenge I’ve been looking for as the next step in my career.

Why I’m here

I want it

“You should listen to the Holy Post podcast. They talk about all this kind of stuff.”

I rolled my eyes deeper than I meant to. At this point, I’d lost count of all the well-meaning Christians trying to send me books, articles, and Bible verses to help along my journey of deconstruction and reconstruction.

While tidbits from the barrage of resources haphazardly thrown at me in an attempt to keep me from losing my way were helpful, I found most to carry the same general sentiment:

“Just pray harder and surrender that which you cannot understand.”

That answer simply would not suffice for someone begging for an honest conversation about the real issues facing the Church today.

My friend didn’t give up on me and sent me an episode on how the Bible is used and abused in politics. Intrigued by the honesty in the title, I hit play. Then I listened to another, and another, and another, and another.

Listening to people wrestle with the same questions I’d once been reprimanded for asking gave me the encouragement and the language I needed to have similar conversations in my real life, with my community and eventually at the beautiful local church I became part of.

I love marketing, genuinely. I love the job I get to do and the career I’ve had. Yet, I cannot ignore the tug on my heart, the small, still whisper that there might be more I have yet to uncover.

Getting to work at Holy Post Media would mean giving more people like me a space to feel hopeful about their faith once again, and I can’t think of a greater mission to be part of than that.

Proof

I’m the gal to do it

From email drip campaigns to billion-dollar brand strategies, I’ve had the privilege to hone my craft from all angles.

Own a function, set priorities, deliver results

As a brand strategist at a small agency, I wore many hats. I owned our research methodology and AI innovation, and set the KPIs that defined what success really looked like for the growth we wanted to achieve. Today, I coach brand teams to set clear priorities and back them with conviction.

Develop and execute campaigns, with strong writing and creative judgment

I build campaigns from the strategy up. I develop omnichannel communications strategy and have written countless communications platforms and creative briefs. I’m also that English nerd who still stays in touch with all my English teachers, because at my core, writing is what has always excited me most.

Confidence working with marketing data

I use data to decide. I analyze sales data, diagnose marketing funnel issues, and lead both quantitative and qualitative consumer research. For an English-loving nerd like me, I find myself still surprised that I enjoy a good spreadsheet. Uncovering what data is really trying to say, telling that story, and acting on it has become one of my favorite parts of every role I’ve been part of.

Email marketing, digital channels, and campaign tools

I’m hands-on across digital channels. As a volunteer, I plan and write email for Jonathan House, a Twin Cities nonprofit, and I ran social media and email marketing in my freelance work. I also publish my own Substack, a platform I feel personally passionate about.

Coordinate colleagues and outside partners, and keep work moving

I lead cross-functional teams and keep the work moving. I believe collaboration and different perspectives only make the work better, and I know what it takes to get a project from start to finish in a matrixed organization. In both agency and corporate settings, I’ve learned to use careful discernment so every partner has their fingerprints on the process and the vision stays intact.

Strategic leadership and the everyday details of execution

Look, someone has to buy the toilet paper and make sure the office printer has ink. Sometimes my leadership has meant pitching in to keep things running smoothly. From the everyday details of running a business to the executional side of marketing, I step in and flex where I’m needed most, whether it’s the 30,000-foot strategic view or the everyday nitty-gritty.

Grow Holy Post Plus

I build strategic positioning and brand foundations that allow sustainable growth to happen. I listen first, identify a strategically aligned target, listen again, and iterate until we get it right. My goal is never just to grow big, but to grow right. I believe my experience in brand and execution can help Holy Post Media find the “right” growth.
